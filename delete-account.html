Delete your Ingletide account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Ingletide and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You'll be signed out and your deletion will begin.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@ingletide.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account." We'll confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your membership in any family group.
  • Your part in session records. Other family members' totals won't include your time anymore.
  • Technical information linked to your account.

How long it takes

We delete your data within 30 days of your request. Copies in our backups are cleared within a further 30 days. If the law requires us to keep something, such as records of a purchase, we keep only that and nothing else.

Deleting the app from your phone doesn't delete your account. Use one of the options above.
