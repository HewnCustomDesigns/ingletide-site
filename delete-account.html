Delete your Ingletide account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Ingletide and go to Settings (the gear on Home).
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete my account again, then Delete to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

If you're in together time, end it first.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@ingletide.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, sign-in and photo.
  • Your People, and the time you've shared with each of them.
  • The moments and photos you added.
  • Nicknames, goals, feedback and anything else linked to your account.
  • Everything Ingletide saved on your phone, when you delete in the app.

Your People keep their time with each other and the moments they added. You'll no longer appear in their Ingletide.

Backups

Copies in our backups are cleared within 30 days of deletion. If the law requires us to keep something, we keep only that and nothing else.

Deleting the app from your phone doesn't delete your account. Use one of the options above.

If you'd just like a break, signing out keeps everything safe until you're back.
