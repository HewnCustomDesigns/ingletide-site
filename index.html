How it works

  1. Gather

    Start a session when your family is in the same room: dinner, a board game, a walk.

  2. Set the phones aside

    Lock your phones and put them down. Ingletide notices they're close together and left alone.

  3. Watch the time add up

    Every shared hour goes into your family's record, so you can see how much time you've really spent together.

An old word for fireside time

Long before screens, families gathered around the hearth at the end of the day. Ingletide is named for that hour.

Fast by an ingle, bleezing finely Robert Burns, Tam o' Shanter (1791)
ingle
A Scots word for a fire burning on the hearth, from the Gaelic for fire and light.
tide
An old word for a time or season, as in Yuletide.
