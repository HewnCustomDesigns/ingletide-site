Present time

Be there for the whole evening.

Lock your phone and set it down, and the time counts as Present. Need to take a call? Unlock and go. Your time picks back up when you lock again. Nothing is ever blocked.

Everyone, the same way

Nobody has to be the phone police.

The phones keep track quietly, so no one has to ask. Everyone plays by the same simple rule, grown-ups included, and nobody is ranked against anyone else.

Any gathering

Room for everyone.

Coffee for two, a double date, the whole family at the table. Anyone nearby with Ingletide can join with one tap.
