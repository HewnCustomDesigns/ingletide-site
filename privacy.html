Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Ingletide is a family app that counts the time you spend together with your phones set aside. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Ingletide is run by an independent developer in British Columbia, Canada, and follows Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Your name, email address and profile picture, from Sign in with Google To create your account and show family members who you are
Your family group: who you've invited and who has joined To know whose time counts together
Session records: when a session started and ended, and who was part of it To build your family's record of time together
Whether your phone stays locked during a session To count only time when phones are set aside
Bluetooth signals from nearby phones running Ingletide To confirm that family members' phones are in the same place. This check happens on your phone; we store only the result, not a map of where you were
Basic technical information, such as app version, phone model and crash reports To find and fix problems

We don't collect your GPS location, contacts, photos, messages or anything you do in other apps. Android may ask for a "nearby devices" permission so the app can use Bluetooth. We use that permission only to detect other Ingletide phones.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: your account, your family group and your shared time.
  • To show your family's sessions to the members of that family group, and no one else.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix bugs.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in system.
  • Google, for Sign in with Google and for app distribution through Google Play.

These providers may store data on servers outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and session records within 30 days. Backups are cleared within a further 30 days.

Your choices

  • See or correct your information. Email us and we'll send you a copy or fix anything that's wrong.
  • Leave a family group at any time from inside the app.
  • Delete your account from the app or by email. See Delete your account.
  • Turn off Bluetooth permission in your phone's settings. Sessions won't be counted without it.

Children

Ingletide accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Your information is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our servers, and access to the database is limited to what the app needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@ingletide.com.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
