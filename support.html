Common questions

Why isn't my session counting?

Check that everyone has Bluetooth turned on, has allowed Ingletide's "nearby devices" permission, and is in the same family group. Phones need to stay locked and close together for the time to count.

Does my phone have to stay unlocked?

No. Lock your phone as usual. Ingletide keeps counting in the background, and unlocking your phone pauses your part of the session.

Will it drain my battery?

Ingletide uses low-energy Bluetooth only while a session is running, so the effect on your battery should be small.

How do I invite someone to my family?

Open your family group in the app and tap Invite. They'll need to install Ingletide and sign in to join.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. Ingletide is launching on Android first.
