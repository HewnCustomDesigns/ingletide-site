Support

Email support@ingletide.com and you'll hear back within two business days. You can also tap Send feedback in the app's Settings.

Common questions

How do I add someone to my People?

Open the People tab and tap the invite button. They can scan the QR code on your screen with the scan button on their People tab, or you can tap Send an invite link. Each invite works once and lasts 7 days. They'll need to install Ingletide and sign in.

How do I join someone's together time?

If one of your People starts together time nearby, your phone will offer to join. You can also ask them to tap Show join code and scan it, which works even if you aren't each other's People yet.

What's the difference between Together and Present?

Together is time your phones were close by. Present is Together time with your phones locked and set aside. You have a minute at the start to lock your phone. Unlocking pauses your Present time, and locking again picks it back up.

Why isn't my time counting?

  • Check that Bluetooth is on and Ingletide is allowed to use it ("Nearby devices" on Android).
  • Allow Ingletide's notifications, so the timer can keep running while your phone is locked.
  • Present time needs a screen lock (PIN, pattern, password or face). Without one, your phone earns Together time only.
  • Your time shows once both phones have saved the session. If someone's phone was offline, it appears after that phone reconnects.

What if I step out of the room?

Up to 5 minutes away still counts. After that you show as stepped away, and after about 30 minutes your part of the session ends.

Will it drain my battery?

Ingletide uses low-energy Bluetooth only while together time is running, so the effect on your battery is small.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes. Ingletide is coming to both Android and iPhone, and Android and iPhone phones count time together.
