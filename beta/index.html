Early testers wanted

Help shape Ingletide before it launches.

We're looking for families, couples and friend groups on Android who spend time together regularly. Try Ingletide for two weeks, then tell us how it went.

  1. Join the tester group

    Use the Google account your Android phone uses for the Play Store, and tap Join group at the top of the page:

    Not signed in? Tap Sign in at the top right first.

  2. Become a tester

    Once you've joined, tap Become a tester on the Google Play page.

  3. Get the app on Google Play

    Install Ingletide from the Google Play Store. If it isn't there yet, wait a few minutes and try again.

Who it's for

  • People who meet in personA household, a couple, roommates, a weekly game night or book club. Anyone you see often.
  • Everyone on AndroidEach person in your group needs an Android phone for now. iPhone testing comes later.
  • Everyone on GmailEach person needs their own Gmail or other Google account. It's how you join the test and sign in to the app.

What you'll do

  1. Join the group

    Tap the first button above. Invite the people you spend time with to join too.

  2. Install the app

    Tap Become a tester on Google Play, then install Ingletide.

  3. Use it for two weeks

    When you're together, tap Start together time, lock your phones and set them aside. Please stay in the test for at least 14 days.

  4. Answer three questions

    A short form at the end. It takes about five minutes.

The three questions

  1. Did the time Ingletide recorded match what really happened?

    If it was ever off, roughly when, and what was going on.

  2. What got in the way of starting a session, or keeping it going?

    Setup, forgetting, battery, a friend without Android: anything counts.

  3. Would you keep using it after the test? Why or why not?

    The why matters most. There are no wrong answers.

Good to know

What is Ingletide?

It keeps a record of the time you spend with the people you love, with phones locked and set aside. Phones sense each other nearby over Bluetooth, and nobody has to be the phone police. See how it works.

What happens to my email address?

It's used only to let you into the test and send you testing updates. You can leave the group any time from its page. See our privacy policy for how the app handles your data.

Will it cost anything?

No. Testing is free.

Something not working?

Email support@ingletide.com, or tap Send feedback in the app's Settings.

Coming soon to your app store

Testers can start today. Everyone else will find Ingletide here at launch.
