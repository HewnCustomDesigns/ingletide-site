Early testers wanted

Help shape Ingletide before it launches.

We're looking for families, couples and friend groups who spend time together regularly. Try Ingletide on your iPhone for two weeks, then tell us how it went.

  1. Get TestFlight

    TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps before they launch. Install it from the App Store.

  2. Join the iPhone test

    Open this page on your iPhone and tap the button. TestFlight opens and asks you to Accept the invite.

  3. Install Ingletide from TestFlight, then open it from your home screen and sign in with Apple or Google.

Who it's for

  • People who meet in personA household, a couple, roommates, a weekly game night or book club. Anyone you see often.
  • iPhone or AndroidThis page is for iPhones. Your group can mix both: people with an Android phone follow the Android steps.
  • An Apple or Google accountEach person signs in with their own. It keeps your time together tied to you.

What you'll do

  1. Get TestFlight

    Install Apple's free TestFlight app, then tap the iPhone test link above.

  2. Install the app

    Install Ingletide from TestFlight. New versions arrive there too.

  3. Use it for two weeks

    When you're together, tap Start together time, lock your phones and set them aside.

  4. Answer three questions

    A short form at the end. It takes about five minutes.

The three questions

  1. Did the time Ingletide recorded match what really happened?

    If it was ever off, roughly when, and what was going on.

  2. What got in the way of starting a session, or keeping it going?

    Setup, forgetting, battery, a friend who couldn't install it: anything counts.

  3. Would you keep using it after the test? Why or why not?

    The why matters most. There are no wrong answers.

Good to know

What is Ingletide?

It keeps a record of the time you spend with the people you love, with phones locked and set aside. Phones sense each other nearby over Bluetooth, and nobody has to be the phone police. See how it works.

Do you see my email address?

Not when you join through the link. TestFlight keeps it private unless you choose to send us feedback. See our privacy policy for how the app handles your data.

Will it cost anything?

No. TestFlight and testing are free.

Something not working?

Take a screenshot in Ingletide and TestFlight offers to send it to us with a note. You can also email support@ingletide.com, or tap Send feedback in the app's Settings.

Coming soon to your app store

Testers can start today. Everyone else will find Ingletide here at launch.
